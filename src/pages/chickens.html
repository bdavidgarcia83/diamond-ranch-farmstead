Delaware chickens

We raise Delaware chickens, an American heritage breed kept for both eggs and meat. You'll know them by their white feathers with black barring on the neck and tail. Our flock ranges with the rest of the ranch under the watch of our guardian dogs.

Laying season

When we're selling eggs, our hens lay all year. In winter they slow down, so orders of more than six hatching eggs can take longer to collect.
