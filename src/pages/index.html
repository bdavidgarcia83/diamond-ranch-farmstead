Guardian dogs and small livestock, raised on the high desert.

We're Ben and Christian Garcia. On our family ranch in Hereford, Arizona we breed Armenian Gampr livestock guardian dogs and raise registered Nigerian Dwarf goats, hair sheep and Delaware chickens.

What we raise

Armenian Gampr

An ancient landrace guardian from the Armenian highlands. Calm with family and stock, serious about predators. We place puppies with working homes.

Litters and waitlist

Nigerian Dwarf goats

Registered dairy goats with full pedigrees. Small, hardy and friendly, with rich milk for a homestead.

Goats for sale

Hair sheep

Low-maintenance sheep that shed their coat, so there's no shearing. Well suited to the Arizona heat.

Sheep for sale

Delaware chickens

A heritage breed for eggs and meat. We sell fresh eating eggs and fertilized Delaware eggs for hatching.
