Sales terms

We go over these terms with every buyer before a deposit is placed. Gampr puppies are also sold under our written Armenian Gampr contract.

Gampr puppies and dogs

Price

  • $3,200 with breeding rights
  • $2,500 as a working guardian or companion without breeding rights. Breeding rights can be purchased later for $700.

AGCA membership and registration

Every buyer must be a member in good standing of the Armenian Gampr Club of America and follow the AGCA Code of Ethics for the life of the dog. New members should join at least one week before pickup. Every puppy must be registered with the AGCA within 30 days of going home. We pay the $25 registration fee for Guardian and Breeder level members, and donate $25 to the AGCA in your name if you're a Lifetime member.

We provide mentorship for the life of your dog.

Deposit

A $500 non-refundable deposit reserves a puppy once we've approved your application, and it's applied to the price. If we can't fulfill your reservation for any reason, we'll refund the deposit right away, or you can move it to a future litter. Microchip details and the remaining health records are provided after the deposit clears.

What comes with your puppy

Puppies are microchipped, vaccinated with two doses of NeoPar for parvo protection plus age-appropriate combination vaccines, and regularly dewormed before pickup.

Pickup, transport and balance

Puppies go home at 8 weeks old, picked up in person in Hereford, Arizona, or by a ground transporter you book and pay for. We do not ship dogs by plane.

  • In-person pickup through 10 weeks: the balance can be paid in full at pickup.
  • Transporter pickup: the full balance is due at least one week before the transporter picks up, so there's time for the vet visit and travel papers. If your transporter requires a health certificate (CVI), add $80.
  • Pickup after 10 weeks: a $75 weekly boarding fee covers food, training and care, the full balance is due before 11 weeks of age, and you cover any extra vet work or vaccinations needed for a CVI.

Payment

We accept Zelle, CashApp, money order or cash. Payments aren't complete until they've cleared, and you're responsible for any app fees. Checks are not accepted.

Health

Each dog is presented at sale as healthy and structurally and temperamentally suited to its job, and we never withhold known health information. We can't guarantee any dog's adult structure, behavior or fertility.

Please have your dog examined by your own veterinarian within 7 days of pickup. If a puppy is found to be in terminal health from a cause that is clearly genetic, confirmed in writing by the veterinarian (we may get a second opinion at our expense), the puppy may be returned to us for a replacement when one becomes available. For older dogs, we refund the purchase price or credit it toward a future dog. The guarantee does not cover neglect or injury after pickup, transport exposure, colds, allergies, parasites, parvo or fertility.

Care and training

Gamprs aren't instant guardians. We ask every buyer to raise their dog underfoot and at their side, the way shepherds do, using only positive reinforcement, and to introduce it to livestock gradually under daily supervision. No e-collars, hitting, yanking or physical correction, and no "toss it in the barn and walk away." Please keep us informed of any major health issues for the life of the dog.

Breeding rights

Dogs sold without breeding rights may not be bred. Breeding rights only take effect once the buyer joins the AGCA Approved Breeder Program as a Merit Breeder and the dog completes:

  • Embark Breed + Health DNA testing within 120 days of going home
  • An AGCA working and physical evaluation after 18 months of age
  • OFA CHIC certification (hips, elbows and patella) after 24 months of age

No outcrossing. Any mate must be AGCA registered, Embark tested, OFA CHIC certified, and listed as an AGCA Approved Breeder's dog. Our contract also sets hip, elbow and DNA-carrier limits on pairings, and we help with raising and placing any litter.

Return to seller

If you can no longer keep your Gampr for any reason, contact us right away. The dog comes back to us at no cost to us, or, if we can't take it at that time, we place it in a home of our choosing under a new contract.

Goats

A $50 deposit holds a goat once we've approved your inquiry, and it's applied to the price. If you need to cancel, tell us why and we'll refund half the deposit ($25). If you don't show up or make no effort to follow through with pickup, the deposit is not refunded.

Goats are picked up at the ranch in Hereford by appointment, and registered goats go home with their registration paperwork or the papers needed to transfer it.

Eggs

Farm fresh eating eggs are $7.00 a dozen, pickup only in Hereford. Fertilized Delaware hatching eggs are $2.50 each and ship by mail; the buyer pays shipping. See the Eggs page for shipping estimates.
