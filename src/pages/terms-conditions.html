Sales terms

We go over these terms with every buyer before a deposit is placed. Gampr puppies are also sold under our written Armenian Gampr contract.

Gampr puppies

Price

  • $3,200 with breeding rights
  • $2,500 as a working guardian or companion without breeding rights. Breeding rights can be purchased later for $700.

Deposit and balance

A $500 non-refundable deposit reserves a puppy once we've approved your application, and it's applied to the price. Microchip details and the remaining health records are provided after the deposit clears. The full balance is due at least one week before pickup.

Payment

We accept Zelle, CashApp, money order or cash.

Breeding rights

Puppies sold with breeding rights must complete these steps before any breeding:

  • Embark DNA testing within 120 days of going home
  • An AGCA working and physical evaluation after 18 months of age
  • OFA hips, elbows and patella evaluations after 24 months of age

No outcrossing. Any mate must be AGCA registered, Embark tested, OFA CHIC certified, and listed as an AGCA Approved Breeder's dog.

Pickup and transport

Puppies can be picked up at the ranch in Hereford, Arizona, by appointment. If you use a transporter, you arrange and pay for ground transport, the health certificate (CVI, about $80) if one is required, and any extra vet work the transporter or a state border requires. We do not fly dogs.

Care and training

We ask every buyer not to use e-collars or physical correction, and not to raise a puppy unsupervised as a "barn only" dog. Please keep us informed of any major health issues for the life of the dog.

Return to seller

If you can no longer keep your Gampr, it comes back to us under the return-to-seller clause in our contract. Please contact us first; we'd rather help than see a dog rehomed without us.

Health
