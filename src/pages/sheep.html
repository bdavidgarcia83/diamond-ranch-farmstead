Dorper Katahdin cross sheep

We raise Dorper and Katahdin cross hair sheep. There's no set percentage of either breed; it's the flock we have right now, and we're breeding and developing it so we can expand onto larger acreage in the future.

Made for the desert

Our sheep do really well browsing and eating weeds, and they're resourceful at making a living on the desert landscape. In winter we do have to supplement their feed. Our Gamprs live with the flock and keep watch over them.

Not for sale right now

We're keeping all of our ewe lambs to grow the flock, and we raise our rams for our own use, so we aren't selling sheep at the moment. We may offer ewes in the future. If you'd like to hear when that happens, send us a note below.
