About the ranch

Diamond Ranch Farmstead is our family ranch in Hereford, Arizona, in the grasslands of the San Pedro Valley.

We're Ben and Christian Garcia. We raise Armenian Gampr livestock guardian dogs, registered Nigerian Dwarf goats and hair sheep, and the animals work together here: our Gamprs guard the herd, and our puppies grow up learning that job from their parents.

We breed for sound, steady guardians and healthy, productive goats. We health test our breeding dogs, keep registration and pedigrees on our goats, and talk with every buyer before an animal leaves the ranch.

We share everyday ranch life as Garcia Roots & Roam on Instagram and YouTube, and post litter and kidding updates on Facebook.
