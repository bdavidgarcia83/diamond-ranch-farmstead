About the ranch

Diamond Ranch Farmstead is our family ranch in Hereford, Arizona, in the grasslands of the San Pedro Valley.

We're Ben and Christian Garcia. On our ranch the animals work together: our Armenian Gamprs live with the goats and sheep and keep watch over them, and our puppies grow up learning that job from their parents.

What we raise

  • Armenian Gamprs, AGCA approved, from health-tested parents. Our breeding dogs are Dozer, Riley, Toros and Athena, who we imported from Armenia.
  • Registered Nigerian Dwarf goats with full pedigrees.
  • Dorper Katahdin cross sheep. We're developing this flock so we can expand onto larger acreage in the future.
  • Delaware chickens, a heritage breed, for farm fresh eating eggs and fertilized hatching eggs.

How we do it

We breed for sound, steady guardians and healthy, productive livestock. We health test our breeding dogs, keep registration and pedigrees on our goats, and talk with every buyer before an animal leaves the ranch.

The rest of the family

The ranch is also home to Nilla, our crossbred guardian; our pet dogs Cody, Butch and Nova; our horses Dakota and Raspberry; and the barn cats. You can meet them all on our Ranch life page.
