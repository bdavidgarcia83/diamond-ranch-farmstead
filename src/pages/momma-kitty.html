Ranch life / Momma Kitty

Momma Kitty

Head of Rodent Control. Retired mother. Full-time Gampr cuddler.

Our Gamprs guard the goats, the sheep and the chickens. Momma Kitty guards nothing, fears nothing, and has made it her life's mission to cuddle every livestock guardian dog on this ranch. So far the Gamprs have not found a way to stop her, and they've mostly stopped trying. Dozer, pictured here, gave up a long time ago.

The job description

Momma Kitty is a professional mouser, and she takes the work seriously. She keeps the rodents in check around the house and the garage. Fewer mice means fewer snakes coming around to look for them, and in southeast Arizona that's a service we're happy to pay for in cat food. She would like it noted that she is the only member of the ranch who works on commission.

How she got her name

Momma Kitty raised a few litters of kittens here, and somewhere along the way "the tabby" became "Momma Kitty." The name stuck. She has since retired from the kitten business and is not taking applications. (We are, though. Just for Gampr puppies.)

A typical day in retirement

  • Rodent patrol around the house and garage. Results speak for themselves.
  • Lounging somewhere on the ranch, in whatever spot is most in the way.
  • Waiting for her moment. The second a Gampr lies down, she's on her way over.
  • Begging to be fed. Any time a human walks by. She will tell you she hasn't eaten in days. She is lying.

The evidence
